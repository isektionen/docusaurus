# Ekonom-I

Ekonom-I is the chapter's central finance group – the hub that holds together all financial work and ensures order and structure in the chapter's finances. The group handles everything from bookkeeping and invoicing to budgeting and follow-up, and ensures that the chapter stands on a stable and transparent financial foundation, which makes it possible for all committees to carry out their projects.

Being part of Ekonom-I is a unique opportunity to work hands-on with finance in an organization with a turnover in the millions. You will learn bookkeeping in Fortnox, understand how budgets are set and followed up, and contribute analyses and reports that influence the chapter's decisions. There is also room for your own initiatives and projects, where you can help develop the chapter's financial work for the future.

As a member, you will among other things handle bookkeeping, take part in budget work and ongoing follow-up, produce reports and analyses for the chapter, support the committees in financial matters, contribute to developing routines and processes, and much more.
